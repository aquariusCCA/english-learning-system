# 各種時式的被動語態

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **一句話核心**
> 被動語態要換時式，**只動前面的 be／助動詞，後面的 p.p. 永遠不變**——簡單式 `be ＋ p.p.`、完成式 `have／has／had ＋ been ＋ p.p.`、進行式 `be ＋ being ＋ p.p.`。

## 🧭 心智圖

```markmap
# 各種時式的被動語態
## 通則
### 主動的各種時式基本上都能改被動
### ⚠️ 未來進行式、三種完成進行式的被動很少見
### 時式落在最前面的 be／助動詞上，p.p. 不變
- He **tells** the truth. → The truth **is told** by him.
## 1-1 簡單式 → be 動詞 ＋ p.p.
### 現在簡單式：am／is／are ＋ p.p.
- Cigarettes **are thought** to cause cancer.
### 過去簡單式：was／were ＋ p.p.
- Many houses **were destroyed** in the flood.
### 未來簡單式：will be ＋ p.p.
- These books **will be thrown out** if no one claims them.
## 1-2 完成式 → have／has／had ＋ been ＋ p.p.
### 現在完成式：have／has ＋ been ＋ p.p.
- The killer **has been arrested** by the police.
### 過去完成式：had ＋ been ＋ p.p.
- By the time the witch appeared, the straw **had been turned into** gold by the strange little man.
### 未來完成式：will have ＋ been ＋ p.p.
- By the time you arrive, a room **will have been prepared** for you.
## 1-3 進行式 → be 動詞 ＋ being ＋ p.p.
### 現在進行式：am／is／are ＋ being ＋ p.p.
- The show **is being watched** by millions of people around the world.
### 過去進行式：was／were ＋ being ＋ p.p.
- The children **were being scolded** by their parents when the visitor arrived.
```

## 📊 圖表解構：八種時式的主動 → 被動

| 時式 | 主動 | 被動 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 現在簡單式 | He **tells** the truth. | The truth **is told** by him. |
| 過去簡單式 | He **told** the truth. | The truth **was told** by him. |
| 未來簡單式 | He **will tell** the truth. | The truth **will be told** by him. |
| 現在完成式 | He **has told** the truth. | The truth **has been told** by him. |
| 過去完成式 | He **had told** the truth. | The truth **had been told** by him. |
| 未來完成式 | He **will have told** the truth. | The truth **will have been told** by him. |
| 現在進行式 | He **is telling** the truth. | The truth **is being told** by him. |
| 過去進行式 | He **was telling** the truth. | The truth **was being told** by him. |

> [!NOTE]
> **注意**
> 各種主動語態的時式基本上都可改成被動語態，不過未來進行式，以及現在完成進行式、過去完成進行式、未來完成進行式的被動語態很少見。

## 📐 用法規則

### 1-1　簡單式的被動語態：be 動詞 ＋ 過去分詞（p.p.）

| 時式 | 結構 |
| --- | --- |
| 現在簡單式 | am／is／are ＋ p.p. |
| 過去簡單式 | was／were ＋ p.p. |
| 未來簡單式 | will be ＋ p.p. |

> Ex.
> - Cigarettes **are thought** to cause cancer. 香煙被認為會致癌。
> - Many houses **were destroyed** in the flood. 許多房子在洪水中被摧毀。
> - These books **will be thrown out** if no one claims them. 這些書如果沒有人認領的話就會被丟掉。

### 1-2　完成式的被動語態：have／has／had ＋ been ＋ p.p.

| 時式 | 結構 |
| --- | --- |
| 現在完成式 | have／has ＋ been ＋ p.p. |
| 過去完成式 | had ＋ been ＋ p.p. |
| 未來完成式 | will have ＋ been ＋ p.p. |

> Ex.
> - The killer **has been arrested** by the police. 這名殺人犯已被警方逮捕。
> - By the time the witch appeared, the straw **had been turned into** gold by the strange little man. 在女巫出現之前，這支稻草已被這名怪異的小矮人變成了黃金。
> - By the time you arrive, a room **will have been prepared** for you. 在你到達前，房間就會幫你準備好。

### 1-3　進行式的被動語態：be 動詞 ＋ being ＋ p.p.

| 時式 | 結構 |
| --- | --- |
| 現在進行式 | am／is／are ＋ being ＋ p.p. |
| 過去進行式 | was／were ＋ being ＋ p.p. |

> Ex.
> - The show **is being watched** by millions of people around the world. 這場表演正被全球各地數百萬人觀賞著。
> - The children **were being scolded** by their parents when the visitor arrived. 這位訪客到達時，這些孩子正被父母教訓著。

## ⚠️ 易錯點分析　💬 AI 補充

> [!WARNING]
> **常見錯誤**
> - ❌ The truth is ~~telling~~ by him.　✅ The truth **is told** by him.（被動只用 p.p.，不用 V-ing、也不用原形）
> - ❌ The killer ~~has arrested~~ by the police.　✅ The killer **has been arrested** by the police.（完成式的被動漏掉 been，句子會變成「兇手逮捕了警方」）
> - ❌ The show ~~is been watched~~.　✅ The show **is being watched**.（進行式的被動用 **being**；been 是完成式在用的）
> - ❌ A room ~~will has been~~ prepared for you.　✅ A room **will have been** prepared for you.（will 後面一律接原形，所以是 have 不是 has）
> - ❌ These books ~~will be threw~~ out.　✅ These books **will be thrown** out.（p.p. 要用第三態：throw–threw–**thrown**、steal–stole–**stolen**、tell–told–**told**）
> - ❌ Many houses ~~was destroyed~~ in the flood.　✅ Many houses **were destroyed** in the flood.（be 動詞跟著**新主詞**的單複數走，不是原本的主詞）
>
> **為什麼錯：** 被動語態是一串固定的零件（be／have ＋ been／being ＋ p.p.），換時式時**只有最前面那個字跟著時式變**，後面的 been、being、p.p. 都是死的。所以三種結構背的其實是「前面接什麼」：簡單式直接 be、完成式 have 系列加 been、進行式 be 加 being。最後再檢查兩件事：p.p. 有沒有用對（不規則動詞的第三態）、be 動詞有沒有跟新主詞一致。
