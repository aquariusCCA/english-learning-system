# 學習驗收　第一章　五大句型

> [!NOTE]
> **關於本篇**
> 這是書中章末的「學習驗收」練習（p.25–26），共兩大題。**題目忠實轉錄自書本**；書中官方解答在**第 526 頁**（本庫未收），下方折疊區另附一份 **💬 AI 分析解答**供自我核對——以官方解答為準，如有出入以書本 p.526 為凖。

## （一）句型判別
> 請指出下列的句子是屬於五大句型的哪一種類型。
> **例：** The children play.（S. Vi.）

- [ ] **1.** The last person in the room turned off the computers and lights.
- [ ] **2.** The excited little boy waited happily by the door.
- [ ] **3.** Andrew's years of experience made him the best choice for the job.
- [ ] **4.** The author gave me a copy of his book.
- [ ] **5.** The new movie about the robots sounds interesting and funny.
- [ ] **6.** The grateful townspeople called the team that stopped the flood great heroes.
- [ ] **7.** The locked trunk lay untouched in the attic for several decades.
- [ ] **8.** The strange little man at the next table was eating a bowl of soup with a fork.
- [ ] **9.** Thomas will have to wake up early in the morning every day next week for work.
- [ ] **10.** The woman who got $7,000,000 in the lottery gave a charity for orphans about half of the money she won.

## （二）句子成分判別
> 請指出下列句子的主詞（S.）、動詞（V.）、受詞（O.）或補語（S.C./O.C.）等。
> **例：** The oldest child looked after her brothers and sisters.（S. V. O.）

- [ ] **1.** He left a big fortune for his wife.
- [ ] **2.** His use of technical terms left his audience confused.
- [ ] **3.** The new president was described as a man of promise.
- [ ] **4.** The director of the company was given complete control of the energy project.
- [ ] **5.** The chef was making his family a special meal.
- [ ] **6.** The battery of tests proved the police chief healthy and fit for duty.
- [ ] **7.** The girl sitting on the bench looked sad and confused.
- [ ] **8.** Regular exercise will keep your body fit and healthy.
- [ ] **9.** My biggest trouble is that nobody knows how I feel now.
- [ ] **10.** Why couldn't you see the people yawning in the audience?
