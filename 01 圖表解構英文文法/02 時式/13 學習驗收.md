<summary>（四）中翻英　解答</summary>

> 以下為常見寫法之一，句意通順即可。

1. Kenny's friends **had (already) started eating** dinner when he finally arrived.
2. **Now that the rain has stopped**, Frank and Tom are going to play baseball.
3. Ethan **is moving to / will move to** Korea next month, where **he will live / will be living** for a year.
4. Michelle **has been studying** for this test all week, and she hopes she does well.
5. According to the schedule, **Angela's flight arrives in twenty minutes**.（時刻表用現在簡單表未來）
6. Susan **was listening to the radio** over the Internet when John knocked on the door.
7. The little dog **had turned around three times before it lay down**.
8. The baseball game **will have (already) ended even before Larry arrives**.
9. The Smith family **has owned this store for more than thirty years**.（own 靜態動詞，不用進行式）
10. Before she **made her first movie**, Jennifer **had been working as** a waitress.
